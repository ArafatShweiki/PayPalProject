## How to Test the Application

1. Open the [Dream Piggy Bank application](https://pay-pal-project.vercel.app/).
2. Click **Continue with PayPal**.
3. Sign in using one of the Sandbox accounts above.
4. Complete onboarding if prompted.
5. Explore the wallet and application features.

### Features to Explore

**1. PayPal Sandbox Funding**

Navigate to **My Wallet → Add Funds** and use a Sandbox Personal account to simulate a PayPal payment.

**2. Smart Savings**

Create a personal savings goal, enable Smart Savings, and simulate purchases to see automatic round-ups allocated to your selected goal.

**3. Multi-Currency Savings**

Explore USD, ILS, and JOD wallets and the application's simulated currency conversion features.

**4. AI Savings Coach**

Explore savings forecasts, spending analysis, and AI-powered recommendations based on recorded activity.

**5. Savings Circles**

Use multiple test accounts to experience collaborative saving:

1. Sign in with Account 1 and create a Savings Circle.
2. Generate a direct invitation link.
3. Open the invitation using Account 2 in a separate browser session.
4. Join the Circle and contribute simulated funds.
5. Use Account 3 to join and contribute as another member.
6. Explore the shared progress and contributor balances.
7. Have the Circle owner request a withdrawal.
8. Test how unanimous contributor approval protects the shared funds.

**6. Financial Reports**

Generate financial reports to review purchases, automatic savings, currency activity, and available AI explanations.

## Important Notes

- All accounts are disposable PayPal Sandbox test accounts.
- All money and wallet activity are simulated.
- No real PayPal funds are transferred.
- Accounts may contain activity from previous demonstrations.
- Multiple testers using the same account will share that account's application data.
- Use separate accounts or browser profiles when testing Savings Circles.
- These accounts do not provide access to PayPal developer credentials or merchant administration.
- External AI and Sandbox services may occasionally be unavailable.
